CORE ARCHITECTURE

Built for Total Sovereignty

Zero cloud telemetry. Zero audio uploads. High-throughput Apple Silicon and NVIDIA acceleration.

⚡

Native Hardware Offloading

Compiles Apple Metal GPU shaders on macOS and CUDA on Linux/Windows. Whisper and Qwen models execute with sub-second response times without heating your CPU.

🔒

Air-Gapped Confidentiality

Meeting audio and confidential negotiations never cross a network wire. All audio capture, Voice Activity Detection, and summary inference happen directly inside your machine.

📝

Structured Minutes & Action Items

Automatically synthesizes full meeting minutes, highlighted decisions, and assigned tasks formatted in rich Markdown. Ready to export, copy, or sync.
