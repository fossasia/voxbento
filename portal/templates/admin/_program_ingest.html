    Recommended OBS output settings
    • Output → Streaming: video encoder x264 or a hardware H.264 encoder (NVENC, QuickSync, Apple VT H.264). Not HEVC or AV1.
    • Keyframe interval: 2 s. Rate control: CBR, at the bitrate YouTube recommends for your resolution (for example 4500–6000 kb/s for 1080p30).
    • Audio: AAC, 128–160 kb/s, 48 kHz.
