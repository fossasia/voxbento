Live source

From a YouTube stream

VoxBento makes captions, translations and translated speech from your stream's sound. It never downloads anything from YouTube: the sound reaches VoxBento from your streaming tool or from a browser tab.

1

Which broadcast?

{% if youtube_connect_enabled %}

Sign in with the YouTube channel and choose the broadcast. VoxBento can then fill in its stream key and put captions in YouTube's CC button. Not your channel, or can't sign in? Go straight to step 2.

{% else %}

Google sign-in is not set up on this server, so VoxBento cannot read your broadcasts or post captions into YouTube's player. Captions, translations and speech still work on VoxBento's listener page: go to step 2.

{% endif %}

3

Share the playing tab with VoxBento

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer that stays on for the whole session.

  1. Open the YouTube stream in a browser tab and start playing it.
  2. Open the sender. It starts on A browser tab; click Start sending, pick the YouTube tab and keep Also share tab audio ticked.
  3. Keep both tabs open. Captions follow the stream as YouTube plays it, so they arrive after YouTube's own viewer delay.
Open sender

The sender tab shows whether sound is reaching VoxBento. Choosing this route switches the room to the sender once you start it there.

✓

Share the listener page

YouTube's CC button carries one caption language. Every language and translated speech are on VoxBento's listener page: put its link in the broadcast description.
