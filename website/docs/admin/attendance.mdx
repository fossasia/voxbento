The **Interpreter attendance** page shows, for every interpreter at your event, when they joined and left
their booth, how long they were there, and how much of that time they were actually interpreting. Use it to
confirm who showed up, plan shifts, and work out paid hours.

Open it from your event page: **Interpreter attendance** in the quick actions. Only event owners and Admins
can see it.

## What you see

- **Totals** for the current view: interpreters, how many are in a booth right now, time in booths and
  interpreting time.
- **One row per interpreter**, with their booth, when they first joined and last left (or *In booth now*),
  their time in the booth and their interpreting time, with a bar for the share of that time.
- **Visits**: expand a row to see each separate visit with its own times.

Interpreters with an account are listed by their account name and email. People who joined with an invite
link are listed by the name they entered, with the link's label.

## Filter and sort

- **Day tabs** show only visits that started on that day. Days and times use your browser's time zone.
- **Booth** shows one room and language.
- **Search** finds an interpreter by name or email as you type.
- Click a column heading to sort by it.

**Download CSV** exports every visit in the current view (one row per visit, times in UTC, durations in
minutes) for a spreadsheet or payroll.

## How time is counted

- **Time in booth** runs from opening the booth page to leaving it. If someone reconnects or has two tabs
  open, each connection is a separate visit, and overlapping visits are counted once.
- **Interpreting** counts only while the person is the booth's active interpreter, their audio is
  connected, and their microphone is on. Muting the microphone in the booth or on the device (a mute
  key, the system's input mute or a headset button the browser reports), handing over to a colleague,
  or Mission Control stopping the broadcast all pause it.

Voxbento saves attendance about once a minute. If the server restarts, open visits end at the last save.
