# Streaming from YouTube

When your event streams on YouTube, VoxBento can add live captions,
translations and translated speech to the stream's audio. The **From a
YouTube stream** option in **Room audio** guides you through three routes
to get audio into VoxBento.

## Which route should I use?

| Setup | How it works | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **OBS / vMix / hardware encoder** | Your encoder sends one stream to VoxBento, which forwards it to YouTube unchanged. | Single-PC setups where one upload matters. |
| **StreamYard / Restream / Zoom** | Your streaming platform sends to YouTube *and* to VoxBento as a second RTMP destination. | Multi-destination platforms that already manage your outputs. |
| **Browser tab audio** | You open the YouTube stream in a browser tab and share that tab's audio to VoxBento. | Streams you don't control, YouTube Studio webcam streams, or quick tests. |

## Why VoxBento cannot take a YouTube URL directly

YouTube's Developer Policy (section III.E.1) prohibits automated
extraction of audio or video streams from YouTube pages. Bot detection
and audio separation would make it unreliable even if it were allowed.
All three routes above give VoxBento the audio legitimately, from the
source side of the stream.

## Setting up the encoder route

1. In **Room audio**, choose **From a YouTube stream**.
2. Under **How does video reach YouTube?**, pick
   **OBS, vMix or a hardware encoder**.
3. Click **Set up forwarding**. You will see a confirmation that says:
   *"While this is on, a VoxBento outage also interrupts your YouTube
   stream."* Accept to continue.
4. Save the YouTube stream key. If you signed in with YouTube in
   Step 1, VoxBento may pre-fill it; otherwise paste the key from
   YouTube Studio (**Go live → Stream → Stream key**).
5. In OBS, open **Settings → Stream**, choose **Custom…** and paste the
   server URL and stream key shown on the page.
6. Start streaming in OBS and check the health rows in **Step 3**.

## Setting up the multi-destination route

1. Choose **StreamYard, Restream, Zoom live streaming**.
2. Click **Enable ingest** to get a server URL and stream key.
3. In your streaming platform, add a **Custom RTMP** destination and
   paste the credentials.
4. Start streaming and check the health rows.

## Setting up browser tab audio

1. Choose **YouTube Studio webcam or a stream I don't control**.
2. Click **Open sender (tab audio)** — the sender page opens in a new
   tab with the tab-audio source preselected.
3. Open your YouTube stream in another browser tab.
4. In the sender, click **Start sending** and pick the tab that has
   your YouTube stream playing.
5. Keep the sender tab open for the whole session.

## Troubleshooting

- **Audio → VoxBento shows "Down"**: Check that your encoder or
  streaming platform is actually sending, and that the server URL and
  stream key are correct.
- **VoxBento → YouTube shows "Down"**: The relay may not be configured
  on this server. Ask the operator to deploy the relay service.
- **No captions appearing**: Make sure captions are enabled on the
  room's Languages tab.
